College Introduction

About The College

Techno International New Town is an engineering and technology institution located in New Town, Kolkata. The college provides undergraduate and postgraduate education in different technical and professional disciplines.
The institution focuses on technical education, practical learning, academic development, and professional skills. Students participate in laboratory work, projects, seminars, and other academic activities.

Basic Informaion

  • Established: 2005
  • Location: New Town,Kolkata,West Bengal
  • Group:Techno India Group
  • University affiliation:Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, West Bengal (MAKAUT)
  • Type:Technical and professional educational institution

Computer Science and Engineering

The department covers programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering, artificial intelligence, and web technologies.

Information Technology

Information technology (IT) is the use of computers, networks, and storage devices to create, process, store, and exchange electronic data

Electronics & Communication Engineering

The department focuses on electronics, communication systems, digital electronics, microprocessors, and related technologies.

Mechanical Engineering

The department covers engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, manufacturing, machine design, and mechanical systems.

Electrical Engineering

The department covers electrical circuits, machines, power systems, control systems, and electrical technology.

Civil Engineering

The department deals with structures, construction, surveying, transportation, environmental engineering, and infrastructure.
